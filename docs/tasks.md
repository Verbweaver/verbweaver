# Tasks User Guide

The Tasks page helps you plan and track your work, such as drafting chapters, revising scenes, or finishing designs. Every page in your project is also a task, so changes you make here appear in the Graph and the Editor too.

Use the buttons at the top to switch between four views: **Board**, **Calendar**, **To-Do**, and **Charts**.

## Board

The Board shows your tasks as cards in columns, such as **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Review**, and **Done**.

- **Create a task:** click **New Task**, or click **+** at the top of a column.
- **Change a task's status:** drag its card to another column.
- **Open a task:** click its card.
- **More actions:** hover over a card and click **⋮** to view details, open the page in the Editor, stop tracking it as a task, or delete it.
- **Change the columns:** click **Manage Columns**. Here you can:
  - add, rename, recolour, reorder, or remove columns;
  - choose the **Default Column** for new tasks;
  - choose the **Completed Column** that finished tasks move to.

Card colours show priority: red for Urgent, orange for High, yellow for Medium, and blue for Low.

## Working on a task

Click a task to see its details. To change it, click the pencil icon, make your changes, then click **Save Changes**. **Cancel** discards your changes.

While editing, you can set:

- the title, description, priority, and assignee;
- start and due dates;
- level of effort;
- status and tags.

You can also:

- **Attach files**, such as reference images or research notes. Click **Upload files**.
- **Link related pages**, such as a character to the chapters they appear in. Click **Create Link**.
- **Add comments** in the panel on the right at any time. Comments save straight away.

To read the page itself, expand **Node Content Preview** or click the page icon to open it in the Editor.

## Calendar

The Calendar shows tasks on their due dates. Switch between **Month** and **Week**, and use **Today** and the arrows to move around.

- **Reschedule a task:** drag it to another day.
- **Create a task due on a day:** click that empty day.
- **Schedule an undated task:** click **Show Unscheduled**, then drag the task onto a day.

## To-Do

The To-Do view is a daily checklist. Pick a day from the strip at the top to see **Today's Tasks** for that day and any **Overdue Tasks**.

- **Mark a task done:** tick its checkbox. It moves to your Completed Column.
- **Reopen a finished task:** untick it and choose which status to move it back to.
- **See finished tasks:** turn on **View Completed**.
- **Give an undated task today's date:** click **Show Unscheduled**, then drag the task into the list.

## Charts

Charts give you an overview of your schedule.

- The **Gantt Chart** shows each task as a bar from its start date to its due date.
- The **Burndown Chart** shows how many tasks are still open over time.

Pick a **Date Range** to focus on a period. Click **Export PNG** to save a chart as an image.

## Filters

On the Calendar and To-Do views, click **Filters** to show only certain statuses or tags. Filters also apply to Charts.

## Tips

- To keep a page out of your task list, choose **Stop tracking as Task** from its menu. The page itself is not deleted.
- **Delete** removes the page from your project entirely.
- Shortcut: **Ctrl+N** (**⌘N** on Mac) creates a new task.
