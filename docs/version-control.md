# Version Control User Guide

The Version Control page saves snapshots of your project as you work. Use it to keep a history of your drafts, undo a change you regret, or try out a different direction without losing your current work.

## Getting started

If you see **Git Not Initialized**, click **Initialize Git Repository** once. This turns on history for the project and saves your current work as the first version.

## Saving a version

1. The **Changes** list on the left shows every page you've added, edited, or deleted since your last saved version.
2. Tick the pages you want to include.
3. Type a short note describing what changed, such as "Finished chapter 3 draft".
4. Click **Commit**.

Click the eye icon next to a page to see exactly what changed in it before you save. The refresh icon updates the list.

## Your history

**Commit History** lists your saved versions, newest first, with the date and your note.

To undo one of those changes, click **Revert** next to it. This adds a new version that reverses that change. Everything else stays as it is.

## Trying another direction (branches)

A branch is a separate copy of your project's history, useful for experimenting, such as an alternate ending.

- Click **+ New Branch**, give it a name, and you'll switch to it straight away.
- Click a branch name under **Branches** to switch to it.
- Save or reset your changes before switching.

## Sharing and backing up

If your project is connected to an online copy, such as one on GitHub:

- **Push** uploads your saved versions.
- **Pull** brings in changes made elsewhere, for example by a collaborator or on another computer.

You can turn on automatic pushing and pulling in **Settings → Collaboration**.

## Starting over

**Reset** throws away every change since your last saved version, including newly added pages. This can't be undone, so use it with care.

## Tips

- Save versions often with clear notes. It makes finding an old draft much easier.
- Shortcuts:
  - **Ctrl+Enter** (**⌘Enter** on Mac) saves a version.
  - **Ctrl+R** (**⌘R** on Mac) refreshes the list.
